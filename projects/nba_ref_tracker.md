## NBA Referee Tracker

Are referees impartial? 

<br>

In professional sports where the stakes are high, referees can single-handedly influence the outcome. Despite this, statistics and data for referee decision is quite lacking if you ask me. While players are analyzed down to every tiny detail, referee performance is nowhere to be found.

<br>

I decided to take this matter into my own hands and so I built this tracker (*disclaimer it's vibe coded with Claude) to add more transparency to how we view referee decisions.

<br>
